Applications for national delegations to **OFM 2027** will open soon. Each
participating French-speaking country will be able to enter a delegation in the
**Junior** (middle school) and **Senior** (high school) categories.

Detailed participation terms, delegation quotas and the registration calendar will
be announced shortly on this official website.
